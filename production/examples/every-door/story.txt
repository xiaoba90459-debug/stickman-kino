Eli opened his apartment door. The hallway was gone.

A newspaper outside said nineteen twelve.

He shut the door, then opened it again.

This time, the same street was dated twenty eighty-nine.

Every door led to a different year.

But closing one erased the way back.

Eli tried to mark his doorway with a key.

The key vanished as the door clicked shut.

He kept going, searching for home.

Then he found his apartment, forty years older.

Someone was already inside.

An older Eli whispered, Do not open the blue door.

Behind him, a blue door began to shake.

The older man was terrified.

Eli stepped back. He finally understood.

The doors were not showing history. They were waiting for his choices.

He chose an empty hallway, and refused to touch its next handle.

For a moment, everything stopped.

Then someone knocked from the other side.

A child asked, Dad? Is that you?
