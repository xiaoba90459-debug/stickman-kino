# Three Seconds Ahead — original story sample

Original character: Mira, coral beret, teal coat, gold scarf, navy trousers, warm ivory face. Fixed proportions and palette in every shot. No reference-video assets used.

| Time | Shot | Visual and action | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–6s | street: The impossible walk | Wide night street. Mira walks into lamplight. Wall shadow turns first; Mira turns exactly three seconds later. | What if your shadow moved three seconds before you did? |
| 6–12s | reaction: Something is wrong | Medium two-shot against a mauve wall. Shadow looks back first. Mira follows, then recoils in surprise. | Mira stopped. Her shadow looked back. Three seconds later, so did she. |
| 12–18s | test: One simple test | Frontal wall test. Shadow raises hand at 0.55s; Mira at 3.55s. Three one-second clock beats make the lead visible. | She raised one hand. The shadow had already done it. |
| 18–24s | danger: A warning, not a mistake | Wide alley. Shadow crouches at 0.55s; Mira at 3.55s. Suspended sign breaks at 4.05s and crashes beyond her. | Then it ducked. Mira followed, just before the sign fell. |
| 24–29s | realization: The close-up | Face and upper-body close-up. Mira looks at the fallen sign, then toward the shadow. Fear softens into understanding. | It wasn't copying her. It was keeping her alive. |
| 29–35s | door: Listen to the warning | Oblique doorway composition. Shadow points away at 0.5s. Mira turns at 3.5s and starts walking back, away from the red door. | At her door, it pointed away. This time, Mira listened. |
| 35–41s | ending: The shadow stays | Wide pull-back. Mira leaves left. Her shadow remains beside the shut door and waves on its own. Hold the unanswered question. | But when she walked away... whose shadow stayed behind? |

The cold-open title and closing question are integrated into the action. No stock intro or subscription outro: the requested runtime is reserved for the story.

Shadow is a second existing rig, flattened to one dark silhouette and staged on the wall. This is a directed 2D projection, not simulated light or a physics system.

Continuity: all movement proceeds right toward the red doorway, then left away from it. The final independent wave intentionally breaks the established anticipation rule.
